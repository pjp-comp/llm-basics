# Nimbus Software — Employee Handbook

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside those hours you may work
whenever suits you, provided you complete 37.5 hours per week. There is no
requirement to be online before 10:00.

## Remote work

All roles are remote-first. You may work from any country where Nimbus has a
legal entity: the United Kingdom, Portugal, India, and Canada. Working from a
country outside this list requires written approval from People Ops, and is
capped at 60 days per calendar year for tax reasons.

## Holiday allowance

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid holiday per year, plus public
holidays in their country of residence. Holiday does not carry over between
years, except for a maximum of 5 days which must be used before 31 March.

Anyone who has been at Nimbus for more than three years receives 2 extra days,
to a maximum of 33 days total.

## Equipment budget

New joiners receive a £2,000 equipment budget for their first year. After that,
the refresh budget is £800 per year, which accumulates for up to three years if
unused. Laptops are replaced on a four-year cycle.

## Expenses

Submit expenses through Ravenna, the internal finance tool, within 45 days of
the purchase date. Anything above £500 requires manager approval before
purchase, not after. Reimbursement is paid with the following month's salary.

## Learning budget

Every employee has £1,200 per year for courses, books, and conferences. It does
not carry over. Conference travel is separate and comes from the team budget.

## Notice period

Notice is one month during the first year of employment, and two months
thereafter. Senior engineers and above serve three months.
